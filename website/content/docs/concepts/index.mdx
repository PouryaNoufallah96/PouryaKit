PouryaKit gives every [Feature](/docs/reference/glossary#feature) the same shape: a command or query, its validator and handler, its data access and its endpoint. The [Kit](/docs/reference/glossary#kit) runs the rest. These pages explain the ideas behind that shape. Each one says what the idea is, the one thing to keep in mind, when it matters to you, and where the Kit's skill states its exact rules.

Read them in order: each page builds on the ones before it. The words in them are the Kit's own, and each links to its entry in the [glossary](/docs/reference/glossary) the first time it appears.

<Callout title="Where the rules live">
  These pages explain and link. The guarantees, options and messages are stated once, in the [Capability](/docs/reference/glossary#capability) pages, which render the Kit's skill. When a page here and a Capability page seem to differ, the Capability page is the rule.
</Callout>
