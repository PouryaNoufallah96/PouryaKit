description: "One activity and one duration measurement for every command and query, and one trace from an Operation through the Outbox into every delivery it causes, in its Host or another."
---

`PouryaKit.Observability` makes every [Operation](/docs/reference/glossary#operation) visible. Each command and query the dispatcher runs is one activity and one duration measurement, tagged with its type, its Actor kind and its outcome, and with its Module in a Host of Modules. The work an Operation publishes stays in its trace: the Outbox carries the trace parent and tracestate into each delivery, so a product created in Catalog and stocked in Inventory is one trace, in one Host or across RabbitMQ.

It emits through `System.Diagnostics` only. The Kit has no OpenTelemetry package, no exporter and no Serilog: exporting is the Host's job, done with the standard OpenTelemetry setup of the [Host recipes](/docs/capabilities/host-recipes).

<Callout type="warn" title="Every Host in the trace registers it">
  A Host that never calls `AddPouryaKitObservability()` emits nothing from the Kit, and its deliveries have no activity. A receiving Host calls it too, or the trace stops at its door.
</Callout>

## When to use it

- You observe the Host's Operations as activities and duration metrics.
- You choose what the Host's exporter listens to.
- You follow one trace from an Operation through the Outbox into its deliveries, in its Host or in another over RabbitMQ.
- You assert telemetry in a test with listeners.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>

### Register it after the dispatcher

`AddPouryaKitObservability()` on the Host, after `AddPouryaKitModules`, wraps the dispatcher. With nothing listening, it adds next to nothing to a dispatch ([Registering](#registering)).

</Step>
<Step>

### Let the activity span the Operation

The activity is current for the whole Operation: authorization, validation, the handler, the Domain Event drain, the Outbox write and the commit. A nested command is a child activity of the one that sent it. Feature code starts no activity of its own.

</Step>
<Step>

### Export from the Host

The Host's exporter listens to the source and meter named by `OperationTelemetry.SourceName`, and to Wolverine's spans when it wants them. The service defaults recipe does this, and exports over OTLP when the endpoint is configured.

</Step>
</Steps>
